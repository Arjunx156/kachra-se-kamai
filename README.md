# Kachra se Kamai — "earn from your waste"

Snap a photo of your dustbin. AI tells you what's in it, what it's worth, and what's headed to Delhi's landfill.
Your recyclables are bundled with your neighbours' and picked up by a local waste picker at a fair, transparent price.

Built for **Environmental Hacks** (Bharat Builds Tour, WeMakeDevs × AWS), Waste and Energy track, Oct 2026.
